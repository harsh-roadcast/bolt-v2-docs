## 3. Problem Statement

White-label branding currently depends on internal teams, Super Admin setup, developer handoffs and repeated support communication. This creates long turnaround times, high operational dependency and inconsistent customer experience.

Customers want to present Bolt as their own branded platform, but they currently lack a guided self-serve configuration surface. Internal teams need to manually collect brand requirements, configure web branding, coordinate Android/iOS builds, share builds for approval, handle repeated changes and track delivery outside a structured product workflow.
