## 16. Communication Branding

Communication branding is part of the broader product direction but is only partially represented in the current approved interface. It should be treated as part of the broader whitelabel roadmap.

### 16.1 Email Branding

Expected configuration:

- Sender name.
- Sender email.
- Reply-to email.
- Logo in email templates.
- Footer links using configured legal URLs.
- Primary brand colour in supported templates.

### 16.2 Email Report Sending

Operational requirements indicate email report sending may involve sender email, monthly email limits, enabled reports and SMTP setup. This should be treated as a dependency with Email/Reports infrastructure.

### 16.3 SMS and Twilio

Operational requirements indicate Twilio has not been widely requested historically and SMS should not be prioritised for initial branding scope. SMS templates remain Phase 2+ unless tied to a broader communication module.
